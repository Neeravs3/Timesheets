Privacy Policy for Timesheets

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Timesheets app handles information. The App is a personal automation script used exclusively by its owner to generate and email PDF reports. It is not a public-facing service.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information from external users. The only data the App processes is the data belonging to the account owner, which is used solely to generate and send the requested PDF reports.

3. How We Use Information

Any data accessed by the App is used only to fulfill its core function: generating a PDF and creating an email draft or sending it via the owner's Gmail account. No data is used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

4. Data Sharing

The App does not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties. All data remains within the owner's Google account and local environment.

5. Data Retention

The App does not maintain a database or long-term storage of user data. Any temporary files (such as generated PDFs) are deleted after the email is sent or the draft is created.

6. Security

The App uses OAuth 2.0 to authenticate with Google services. Credentials are stored locally in a token file and are never transmitted to any external server.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app owner at neerav.saxena@gmail.com.
